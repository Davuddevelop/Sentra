Questions parents ask

The answers you deserve, clearly.

Does Sentra read my child's private messages? +
No. Sentra analyzes behavioral patterns, metadata, and app-level signals — never the content of private conversations. This is a core design principle, not a marketing claim. Our on-device AI models are specifically designed to detect concerning patterns without needing message content.
Will my teenager know Sentra is installed? +
Yes — and that's by design. Secret surveillance damages trust, and trust is what actually keeps kids safe. Sentra has a child-facing view so they understand what's monitored and why. Research consistently shows that transparency leads to better family outcomes than covert monitoring.
What makes this different from Circle, Bark, or Qustodio? +
Three things: AI-native monitoring (we were built from the ground up for AI chatbot safety), behavioral pattern detection (we don't read content), and a child-transparent model (kids can see what's monitored, which builds cooperation rather than resentment). Legacy tools were built for a different internet.
Is it really private if you're analyzing behavior? +
Yes. The signals the extension collects are behavioral metadata — session durations, signal type, timing — never message content. AI risk scoring runs on our server using anonymized signal data. What we store cannot be used to reconstruct what your child said.
What age is Sentra for? +
Sentra is designed for children aged 6–17. Protection profiles adapt automatically based on age: a 7-year-old's experience looks very different from a 16-year-old's, with different alert thresholds, different dashboard views, and different amounts of autonomy given to the child.
Can I cancel anytime? +
Yes. No contracts, no cancellation fees. Cancel from the app in under 30 seconds. If you cancel within 30 days of a paid plan starting, we'll refund you in full — no questions asked.
