    Step 3 of 3

    Install on their browser.

    Install the Sentra extension on your child's Chromebook or desktop Chrome. It takes 60 seconds.

    1
    Open Chrome on your child's device and go to chrome://extensions
    2
    Enable Developer mode (top-right toggle), then click Load unpacked
    3
    Click the Sentra extension icon and paste your device token to connect
    4
    Set a PIN in extension settings so your child cannot remove it
