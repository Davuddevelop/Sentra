Sentra

Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Sentra analyses behavioral patterns, never message content. No AI conversation text ever reaches our servers — only the signal type and a risk score.

1. What we collect

Sentra collects behavioral metadata only — never the content of any AI conversation. Specifically, we collect:

  • Signal type (e.g. "jailbreak attempt detected", "long session")
  • App name (e.g. "ChatGPT", "Character.AI")
  • Session duration in minutes
  • Pattern flags (e.g. "romantic keyword detected") — not the keywords themselves
  • Device name and platform (browser, OS)
  • Timestamp of each signal

All pattern matching happens on-device inside the browser extension. Only the result (a signal type) is transmitted — never the matched text.

2. What we never collect

  • The content of any message sent to or received from an AI chatbot
  • Browsing history outside monitored AI platforms
  • Keystrokes, screenshots, or screen recordings
  • Passwords, personal documents, or financial information
  • Precise location data

3. COPPA compliance (children under 13)

Sentra is designed for use by parents to monitor their minor children. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA):

  • Parents must provide verifiable consent before monitoring begins
  • We collect only the minimum behavioral metadata necessary
  • No data about children is sold or shared with third parties for advertising
  • All data is automatically deleted after 90 days
  • Parents may request deletion of all data at any time by emailing privacy@sentra.app

4. Data retention

Behavioral signals and alerts are automatically purged after 90 days. Account data (name, email) is retained until account deletion. You may delete your account at any time from the dashboard settings.

5. Third-party services

Sentra uses the following third-party services:

  • Anthropic Claude API — used to analyse behavioral metadata and generate alert descriptions. Metadata (signal type, session duration, app name) may be sent to Anthropic. No message content is included. Anthropic's privacy policy applies.
  • Resend — used to send alert notifications and weekly digests to parents. Your email address is shared with Resend for delivery purposes only.
  • Stripe — used to process subscription payments. Payment card data is handled entirely by Stripe and never touches our servers. Stripe's privacy policy applies.

6. Your rights (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area, you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict processing. Contact us at privacy@sentra.app.

7. Contact

For any privacy questions or data deletion requests: privacy@sentra.app
